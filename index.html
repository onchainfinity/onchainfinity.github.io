Artistic approach

Memory, living matter.

Onchainfinity is a digital artist working with the memory of artworks.

Paintings, poems and scientific texts provide the raw material. AI analysis, code and the artist’s choices open passages between forms: from image to sound, from text to imagined world.

Each project follows its own path. All seek to make audible or visible what endures of a work as it transforms.

Studio notes
01On the essence of a work

To seek the essence of a work is not to summarise it. It is to attend to what remains within us: a colour, a tension, a rhythm, an emotion. The creation that emerges does not replace the original; it invites us to hear or see it differently.

02The machine and the hand

The machine multiplies possibilities. It remains for the artist to choose a path, recognise what rings true, and let go of what dissipates the emotion. The work takes shape in this dialogue, as much through what the artist keeps as through what they leave out.
