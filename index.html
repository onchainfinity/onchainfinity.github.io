Behind the works

Memory is not still.

Onchainfinity explores what a work can become after it has already lived.

A poem, a painting, a scientific text: each holds more than its original form. Through human interpretation, artificial intelligence and code, these traces become music, images and imagined worlds.

Not every project follows the same path. What connects them is a desire to make the memory of human culture perceptible in another form.

Correspondences

Notes from the practice
01On the essence of a work

To extract an essence is not to summarise a work. It is to listen to what it leaves behind: an atmosphere, a tension, a movement of thought. The new work does not replace its source. It opens another way of approaching it.

02The machine and the hand

The machine does not replace the hand. It enlarges the labyrinth in which the hand must choose. An intention, a selection, a refusal: the work also takes shape in everything that is not retained.
