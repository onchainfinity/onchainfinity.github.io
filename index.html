Velvet Index / Album coming soon

1984

Intimacy under surveillance.

With 1984, I move through George Orwell’s novel in search of what still resists: desire, memory and tenderness. Between modern new wave and electronic post-punk, the album is taking shape; Golden Country opens the first passage.

Velvet Index is an imagined band created with artificial intelligence, under Onchainfinity’s artistic direction.

Artistic approach

To dwell within art.

I create from the memory of artworks, for the pleasure of rediscovering them and experiencing them anew, intimately.

In AI, I seek the traces of our humanity: our knowledge, our dreams, our beauty and our contradictions. Code and my choices give them new forms, through images, books, music and digital worlds.

Wonder does not dispel unease about tomorrow. For now, I seek beauty there, while there is still time.
